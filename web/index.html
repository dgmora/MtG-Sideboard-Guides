Magic: The Gathering

Where to keep your guides

Best: start from a copy of the template and keep one spreadsheet with a tab per deck. The tab name is the deck name. Share it as "Anyone with the link can view" and add its link above: every tab becomes a guide. Edit the sheet, come back to this page and the card updates.

Guides in several spreadsheets? Add one link per line. Or select the cells in Google Sheets/Excel and paste them under Paste.

Everything happens in your browser: we never see or store your spreadsheet. This page only remembers your links, on this device.

How to lay out each tab

mdsbCardBurnStormElves
3Flickerwisp-2
2Mirran Crusader-2
4Thalia-1
2Path to Exile+1*
2Kor Firewalker+2
2Canonist+2
  • md / sb: copies in the main deck and sideboard.
  • One column per matchup: cards in (+) and out (−). Add * for an optional swap.
  • Empty row: everything below it is sideboard.
  • Empty column: splits the matchups across the two halves of a folded card. Guides with many matchups split on their own.

Mostly no validation: the only check is a warning when a matchup brings in more cards than it takes out. Anything that doesn't fit this layout is skipped or shown as-is.
